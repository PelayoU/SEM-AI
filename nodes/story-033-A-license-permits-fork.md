# Story 033-A — LICENSE legally permits forks

> Parent: [[feature-033-license-permits-forks]].

## Cohn statement

As a **new-project author or external fork author**, I want **the LICENSE to explicitly permit copying the substrate to a new project + derivative works**, so that **portability is legally clean rather than implicit**.

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- License is OSI-approved (or equivalent permissive).
- Terms explicitly permit copying, modification, derivative works.
