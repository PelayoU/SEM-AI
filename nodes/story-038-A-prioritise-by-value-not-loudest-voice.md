# Story 038-A — Prioritise by quantitative value, not political volume

> Parent: [[feature-038-po-value-analysis-skill]].

As a **PO with competing capabilities or features**, I want **a quantitative value analysis (10 tangible + 9 intangible items per Jones BP #18)**, so that **prioritisation is value-weighted rather than driven by the loudest voice**.

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- Both tangible + intangible buckets analysed.
- ROI or value-points-per-cost computed against an alternative.

✅ I · ✅ N · ✅ V · ✅ E · ✅ S · ✅ T (applied when prioritising backlog).
