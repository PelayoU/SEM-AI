# Story 050-A — Seven fundamental topics as audit grid

> Parent: [[feature-050-architect-architecture-design-skill]].

As an **Architect designing or auditing**, I want **every architectural decision walked against Jones's seven fundamental topics (structure / data / interfaces / decomposition / linkage / performance / security)**, so that **silence on a topic is structural rather than accidental** (Jones identifies it as the most common architectural defect).

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- ADR / design artifact addresses all 7 topics explicitly (or "not applicable" with reason).
- Audit checklist available to inspector.

✅ I · ✅ N · ✅ V · ✅ E · ✅ S · ✅ T (10 ADRs in Phase 1 demonstrate the grid).
