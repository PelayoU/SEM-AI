# Story 068-A — 220-defect multiplier explicit in support sizing

> Parent: [[feature-068-devops-customer-support-skill]].

As **DevOps sizing support**, I want **the 220-defect-reduction ≈ 1 support FTE multiplier explicit when negotiating with PO and QA**, so that **the trade-off between upstream quality investment and downstream support cost is visible during decision-making, not after the fact**.

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- Support sizing document carries the multiplier inline.
- QA-investment-vs-support-cost trade-off surfaced to PO at planning time.
