# Story 064-A — Renovate legacy before major enhancement

> Parent: [[feature-064-developer-maintenance-skill]].

As a **Developer about to enhance aging legacy**, I want **renovation first (complexity analysis + error-prone-module surgery + dead-code removal)**, so that **the enhancement doesn't inherit the legacy's complexity and defect debt** (per Jones BP #28 practice 12 + BP #48).

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- Renovation plan precedes enhancement plan.
- Complexity above ceiling refactored before new feature work.
