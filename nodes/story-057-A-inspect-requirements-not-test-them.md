# Story 057-A — Requirements defects caught at requirements inspection

> Parent: [[feature-057-qa-inspections-program-skill]].

As **QA running the inspection program**, I want **requirements defects caught at requirements inspection (not deferred to testing)**, so that **Y2K-style defects don't flow downstream because no test form can find them**.

## Conditions of Satisfaction

- Requirements inspection scheduled in milestone plan.
- Fagan 5 preconditions met (moderator / recorder / prep time / defect log / no appraisal use).
