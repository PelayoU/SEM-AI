## Horizon (slide 150)

> Declared in frontmatter as `horizon:` — one of:
> - **roadmap** (1–2 years; focus: vision / product evolution)
> - **release** (2–9 months; focus: best value within constraints)
> - **iteration** (1–4 weeks; focus: features deliverable now)

- Why this goal? → <answer 1>

## SMART self-check (slide 95, attributed to Doran 1981)

Mark ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ with one-line reason:

- **S — Specific:** population, scope, outcome bounded.
- **M — Measurable:** numeric or boolean check defined.
- **A — Achievable:** hittable within the declared horizon given current team velocity.
- **R — Relevant:** advances the parent vision; would survive vision-retirement test.
- **T — Time-bound:** deadline implied by the horizon.
